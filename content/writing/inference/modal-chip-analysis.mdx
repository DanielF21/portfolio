## Notes

- **T4 has no BF16.** Turing supports FP16 tensor cores but not BFloat16.
- **T4 bandwidth is 300 GB/s** per NVIDIA's primary T4 datasheet. NVIDIA's
  virtualization datasheet states "up to 320 GB/sec", which is the figure
  most third party spec sites repeat.
- The T4's 65 TFLOPS is quoted by NVIDIA as "mixed precision (FP16/FP32)",
  meaning FP16 multiply with FP32 accumulate, the same operation the other
  rows list as FP16 Tensor Core.
- **A100 40GB** exists in SXM4 and PCIe forms, both 1,555 GB/s at 40 GB.
- **L40S, L4 and A10 are GDDR6, not HBM.**
- FP32 column is the non tensor core CUDA core rate. fp16 and bf16 inference
  uses the tensor core path.
